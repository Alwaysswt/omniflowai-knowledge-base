## 🏢 About OmniFlow Software & AI

OmniFlow Software & AI is a Vancouver-based technology and creative production studio. We specialize in three core capability areas:
1. **Local Business Growth & Online Presence**: High-converting websites, Local SEO, and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO).
2. **Commercial AI Video & Film Studio**: Cinematic brand films, property visual showcases, institution reels, and vertical brand dramas.
3. **Custom Software & AI Automation**: Tailored business platforms, internal operational tooling, and rapid MVP delivery.

## 🚀 Three Core Service Lines

### 1. Local Business Growth (本地商家获客与数字化)
Targeted at retail, dining, professional practices, and home services in Metro Vancouver:
- High-performance, mobile-first responsive business websites.
- Structured data (Schema.org / JSON-LD) for Local Business visibility.
- Generative Engine Optimization (GEO): Ensuring business details and offerings are accurately represented in AI answers (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Apple Intelligence).
- Clear consultation and inquiry tracking channels.

### 2. Commercial AI Films & Video Studio (商业 AI 影像工坊)
Led by experienced production professionals with industry-grade visual effects backgrounds:
- **Cinematic Brand & Commercial Films**: High-impact storytelling for enterprises and local brands.
- **Institutional & Campus Spotlights**: Comprehensive visual presentation (e.g., promotional film production for *D&H College*).
- **Branded Vertical Short Dramas**: Story-driven brand marketing for restaurants and lifestyle brands (e.g., vertical branded short drama for *Shunfeng Shunshui Restaurant Group*).
- **Diverse Commercial Visuals**: Architectural concepts, culinary showcases, educational promos, and private legacy documentaries.
- *Online Brief & Checklist Tool*: [https://omniflowai.ca/checklist/ai-drama](https://omniflowai.ca/checklist/ai-drama)

### 3. Custom Systems & AI Automation (定制软件与业务系统)
Designed for growing businesses needing connected workflows rather than rigid SaaS subscriptions:
- Cloud-native business platforms tailored to operational bottlenecks.
- LLM and workflow automation pipelines (document parsing, multi-channel customer inquiry triage, automated notifications).
- Agile MVP validation: Core capability prototyping in ~15 business days to validate operational flows before enterprise-scale buildout.
